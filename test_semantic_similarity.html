🔬 Core Function Tests

🔒 HARD Thesis Isolation

Threshold: 0.35 - Any content with similarity > 0.35 to the central thesis is automatically eliminated before role assignment.

  • Prevents thesis duplication across evidence, mechanisms, and contrasts
  • Applies to ALL modes with strict isolation
  • Ensures thesis appears exactly once per output

🎯 Quiz Option Distinctiveness

Threshold: 0.25 - Multiple choice options must be pairwise distinct with very strict similarity checking.

  • Every option compared against all other options
  • Similar options (>0.25 similarity) are eliminated
  • Fallback to generic distractors if insufficient distinct options

📋 Test Scenarios

1. Thesis Isolation Test

Central thesis: "Climate change is primarily caused by human activities"

Similar content that should be eliminated: "Human activities are the main cause of climate change"

2. Quiz Option Distinctiveness Test

Options that should be filtered out due to high similarity:

  • "Climate change results from human activities"
  • "Human activities cause climate change"
  • "Climate change is caused by humans"

3. Cross-Role Deduplication Test

Content appearing in multiple roles should be consolidated or eliminated based on mode-specific thresholds.
